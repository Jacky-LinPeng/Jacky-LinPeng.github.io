Privasi

Berlaku: 17 Oktober 2026 · Pengembang: Peng Lin · 540933120@qq.com

Learning and recordings

No account is required. Teaching language, goals, Russian lesson progress, review history, flowers, desk decorations and reward receipts are stored locally. We do not operate a server receiving this learning data.

Optional voice practice uses the iPhone microphone and Russian system speech. Temporary recordings remain on the device, are deleted when practice closes or the app enters the background, are not uploaded and receive no pronunciation score. Offline speech depends on installed voices.

Advertising and consent

RusQuest does not ask for an age declaration. Google AdMob banner, app-open, interstitial and optional rewarded ads may be requested only when Google UMP reports that requests are permitted under the current privacy state. Every lesson has a free learning unlock route. Each rewarded ad requires a separate choice; only an earned-reward callback grants the disclosed lesson and/or 10 flowers. Ad availability is not guaranteed.

Google Mobile Ads and User Messaging Platform may process IP address (including approximate location), device identifiers, advertising data, product interactions, crashes and performance for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. We disable publisher first-party identifiers and personalization, request Limited Ads using Google's documented device-storage restriction, and do not request IDFA or tracking permission. This does not mean the SDK collects no data. Google UMP manages consent where required. See Google Privacy and Google Advertising.

Control and retention

Exported backups go only to the location you choose. Import validates RusQuest identity and integrity before replacing progress. Reset clears lessons, reviews, flowers and desk decorations while retaining local reward receipt history to prevent duplicate rewards and recover earned pending transactions.

Uninstalling removes local app data; you control exported copies. We do not sell local learning records or provide social sharing or public profiles. Do not send recordings or sensitive information in support messages. RusQuest is a beginner Russian supplement and is not directed at children.

学习与录音

无需注册账户。教学语言、目标、俄语课程进度、复习记录、红花、书桌装饰和奖励回执保存在本机；临时语音录音不上传，并在离开练习或进入后台时删除。

广告与同意

RusQuest 不询问年龄声明。只有 Google UMP 根据当前隐私状态报告允许请求时，应用才会请求 Google AdMob 横幅、开屏、插屏和自愿激励广告。所有课程都有免费学习解锁路线；只有有效奖励回调才发放已披露奖励。

Google Mobile Ads 和 User Messaging Platform 可能为广告、衡量和反欺诈处理 IP 地址、设备标识符、广告数据、产品交互、崩溃和性能资料。我们关闭发布商第一方标识符和个性化，不请求 IDFA 或追踪权限。参见 Google 隐私政策。

控制与保留

导出备份只发送到你选择的位置；重置会清除学习进度，但保留本机奖励回执以防重复发奖。卸载会删除应用本地数据。
