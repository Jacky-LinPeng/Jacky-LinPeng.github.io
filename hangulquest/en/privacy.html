Privacy policy

Privacy policy — HangulQuest
Effective: October 17, 2026
Developer: Peng Lin. Support: 540933120@qq.com.

Learning and recordings
No account is required. Teaching language, goals, lesson progress, review history, flowers, decorations and reward receipts are stored locally on your device. We do not operate a learning server that receives them. Voice practice uses Korean system speech. Microphone permission is optional. Practice recordings are temporary local files, deleted when practice closes or the app moves to the background, never uploaded and never scored.

Advertising and consent
Google AdMob banner, app-open, interstitial and optional rewarded ads may be requested after the required privacy flow allows ad requests. The app does not collect or send an age declaration. Every lesson also has a free learning route. A reward is granted only after a separate voluntary choice and the SDK's earned-reward callback. Google Mobile Ads and User Messaging Platform may process IP address and approximate location, device identifiers, advertising data, product interactions, crash data and performance data for delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. We disable ad personalization and publisher first-party identifiers and do not request IDFA or tracking permission. Limited Ads can still use device storage for invalid-traffic detection. Consent choices are managed with Google UMP where required. See https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads .

Control and retention
Export a progress backup before uninstalling if you want to keep it. Import validates a file before replacing progress. Reset clears learning, flowers and the garden; local reward receipt history is retained to prevent duplicate awards and recover earned pending transactions. Uninstalling removes app-local data. Exported copies remain under your control. We do not sell local learning records and provide no social sharing or public profile. Contact the developer for support or privacy questions; do not send sensitive information or recordings.

HangulQuest is a beginner Korean supplement and is not directed at children. This policy is updated if data practices change.
