Privacy policy

BonjourQuest teaches beginner French through 30 built-in lessons. Learning progress, preferences, rewards, and optional practice recordings stay on this device unless you explicitly export a backup. Microphone access is optional; recordings are used only for local playback, are not uploaded, receive no pronunciation score, and are deleted after practice. Ads may use Google Mobile Ads and the User Messaging Platform according to your privacy choices; ads require a network connection, while lessons remain available offline. BonjourQuest does not require an account or sell your learning data. Do not include personal information or recordings in support messages.
