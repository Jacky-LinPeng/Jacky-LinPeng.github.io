Progress, audio and advertising help

Export a progress backup before uninstalling. An invalid or unsupported import never replaces your current data.

If speech is silent, install a French voice in iPhone Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content. Recording is optional, stays on your device and never produces a pronunciation score.

If no ad is available, keep learning for free. When reporting an ad issue, include the app version, approximate time and ad format, but no personal data or recordings.
