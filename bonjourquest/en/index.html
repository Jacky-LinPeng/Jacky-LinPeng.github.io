BonjourQuest: Learn French

Everyday French through small quests

Practice 300 French words, 30 useful patterns and 30 short dialogues across a café, station and market.

Lessons, progress, review and the garden work offline. System French voices must be installed for offline speech.

Score 80% in an independent check to open the next lesson in the same scene. Every lesson can be unlocked by learning; an ad reward is only an optional shortcut when available.

Flowers are progress rewards for your garden and have no cash value. No account or subscription is required.

Supplementary beginner practice; not a proficiency certificate or a guarantee of travel, study or work outcomes. Audio uses system speech.
