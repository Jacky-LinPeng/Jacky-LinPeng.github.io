Privacy policy

Privacy policy — VietToneQuest: Vietnamese Tones
Effective: October 17, 2026
Developer: Peng Lin. Support: 540933120@qq.com.

Learning and recordings
No account is required. Language, age eligibility, goals, lesson progress, review history, flowers, decorations and reward receipts are stored locally on your device. We do not operate a server that receives them. Voice practice uses the iPhone system microphone and Vietnamese speech synthesis. Microphone permission is optional. Practice recordings remain temporary local files and are deleted when practice closes or the app moves to the background. They are not uploaded and no pronunciation score is generated. System speech availability offline depends on installed voices.

Advertising and consent
Eligible adults may receive Google AdMob banner, app-open, interstitial and optional rewarded ads. Unknown age and under-18 choices disable advertising requests. All lessons also have a free learning unlock route. Rewarded ads require a separate choice each time; only an earned-reward callback grants the disclosed lesson and/or 10 flowers. Ad availability is not guaranteed.
Google Mobile Ads and User Messaging Platform may process IP address (including approximate location), device identifiers, advertisement data, product interactions and diagnostics such as crashes and performance for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. We disable publisher first-party identifiers and ad personalization, and do not request IDFA or tracking permission. This does not mean the SDK collects no data. We request Limited Ads using Google’s documented device-storage restriction. Limited Ads disables personalization and features that rely on local advertising identifiers; programmatic demand may still use storage solely for invalid-traffic detection. Google’s consent form may use English where your teaching language is unavailable; this policy and the app’s privacy controls are available in all four teaching languages. Consent choices are managed using Google UMP where required; open My town → Privacy choices to review available options. Refusing a choice does not remove basic lessons.
See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy and advertising information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads .

Control and retention
Export a progress backup from My town before uninstalling if you want to keep it. Backup files are sent only to the location you select. Import validates the file before replacing learning progress. Reset clears learning, flowers and garden on this device; reward receipt history is retained locally to prevent duplicate rewards and to recover already earned pending transactions. Uninstalling removes the app's local data. You control copies of exported backups. Contact the developer for support or privacy questions; do not include sensitive information or recordings in ad feedback. No sale of local learning records, no social sharing and no public profile are provided.

This app is a beginner Vietnamese supplement and is not directed at children. We update this policy if data practices change.
