Privacy policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

This policy describes Lingo Isles (com.linpeng.lingoisles), offered by Peng Lin. For privacy questions, contact linpeng.dev@gmail.com.

1. Learning on your device

No account is required. Your age group, chosen language and stage, answers, course progress, favourites, practice history and rewards are stored in the app on your device. We do not ask for your date of birth or real name. The app has no developer-operated learning-data server or cross-device sync. Local recovery copies may be retained. These files may be included in iOS device backups.

2. Microphone and speech

The microphone is used when you start recording and grant permission. Temporary recordings support playback and are normally removed when replaced or when you leave a lesson. An interrupted session may leave temporary files until they are cleared. Optional transcription runs only when on-device recognition is supported; the app does not fall back to cloud recognition or send recordings to our server. Transcribed answers may be saved with local lesson progress. Listening practice works without microphone permission. Examples use system-generated speech; no pronunciation score is provided.

3. Advertising services

For eligible adult profiles, Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform provide advertising and privacy choices. Google may process IP addresses (including approximate location derived from them), device identifiers, ad impressions and interactions, and diagnostic or performance information for advertising, measurement, security and service operation. The app requests non-personalised ads and disables publisher first-party identifiers; this does not mean that no data is processed. Applicable privacy choices are checked before ad requests. Google determines its own retention periods and may process information outside your country. See Google's privacy policy for its practices. Adults must allow tracking in the iOS permission prompt before the app starts advertising and regional privacy services. Advertising partners may use device identifiers to measure ads across apps and websites. Declining or restricting permission does not limit learning. You can change this permission in iOS Settings; withdrawing it stops new app-initiated ad requests and clears pending ads, but cannot unload an already started SDK or delete data already processed by Google.

4. Younger learners

An unknown age group or a profile under 18 does not initiate the consent flow, advertising SDK initialisation or new ad requests. Switching from an adult profile stops new app-initiated ad requests and clears pending ads; it cannot unload a previously started SDK or delete data already processed by Google. Choose the appropriate age group when setting up the app.

5. Your controls and retention

You can manage microphone and speech permissions in iOS Settings. Where required, advertising privacy options appear in the profile screen. Clear learning data resets local progress and preferences, recovery files and temporary recordings; this cannot be undone through our support service. Local learning data otherwise remains until you clear it or remove the app, subject to iOS backup behaviour. Clearing learning data does not withdraw Google's consent choices or delete data held by Google.

6. Contact and requests

If you email support, your email address and the information you choose to send are processed by the email service and used to answer your request. Do not send passwords, payment details or recordings. We keep support correspondence only as needed to resolve and follow up on the request or meet applicable obligations. Contact linpeng.dev@gmail.com about access, correction, deletion or other privacy requests. We cannot look up device-only learning progress by email address. Requests about Google's data can also be made through Google's privacy controls.

7. This website and policy updates

These pages do not use analytics, advertising scripts, tracking cookies or web forms. If you visit a hosted version, its hosting provider may process IP addresses and request logs to deliver and secure the site. External links and email services have their own privacy practices. Changes to this policy will be published on this page with an updated date.
