A little help for your next step

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Need help with Lingo Isles? Contact linpeng.dev@gmail.com. Include your device model, iOS version, app version, interface language, and the steps that led to the problem. Screenshots should not contain sensitive information.

I cannot hear the example

Check the device volume and audio output, and make sure an English system voice is available. Examples use system-generated speech, so voice availability depends on device settings.

Can I practise without a microphone?

Yes. Choose the listening alternative. Recording is optional; on-device transcription also needs permission and a compatible device. Manage these permissions in iOS Settings.

Why is there no pronunciation score?

Speaking practice records and replays your attempt. Optional transcription shows recognised text, not a pronunciation grade.

Why are explanations in English?

The interface supports 16 languages. Learning explanations are currently available in English and Simplified Chinese. Missing translations are clearly shown in English.

Where is my progress?

Progress is stored on this device. There is no account-based cloud sync. A recovery notice means a local backup may have been restored and recent actions may need to be repeated.

How do I delete my learning data?

Open the profile settings, choose Clear learning data, and confirm. This also clears local recovery copies and temporary recordings. Support cannot undo this. Advertising privacy choices are managed separately.

How do ads and rewards work?

Unknown-age and under-18 profiles do not start advertising. Adult ad availability depends on privacy choices and network availability. Reward ads are optional and rewards are granted only after a qualifying completion callback; unavailable ads do not grant rewards.
